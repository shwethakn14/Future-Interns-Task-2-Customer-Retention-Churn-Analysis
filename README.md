# Future Interns Task 2 – Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 2**.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer retention and churn patterns, understand customer lifetime behavior, identify factors associated with churn, and provide practical recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- GitHub

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard contains two main analysis pages:

### 1. Churn Overview

The Churn Overview dashboard analyzes:

- Total customer count
- Overall churn rate
- Churn by Internet Service
- Churn by Online Security
- Churn by Tech Support
- Churn by Payment Method
- Customer distribution by Monthly Charges

### 2. Customer Lifetime Analysis

The Customer Lifetime Analysis dashboard focuses on:

- Customer tenure
- Customer tenure and churn
- Monthly charges
- Customer lifetime patterns
- Customer distribution across different tenure periods
- Relationship between tenure and churn

## Key Insights

- The overall customer churn rate is approximately **26.54%**.
- Customers with shorter tenure show higher churn compared with longer-tenure customers.
- Customer tenure is an important indicator of customer retention and lifetime behavior.
- Monthly charges help identify customers with different spending levels.
- Customers with different Internet Service, Online Security, and Tech Support options show different customer and churn patterns.
- Payment methods also show differences in customer distribution and churn behavior.
- Customers with higher monthly charges may require focused retention strategies because losing these customers can have a greater revenue impact.

## Recommendations

1. Focus on customers during their early months of service, as they show higher churn risk.
2. Provide special offers or incentives to customers who are at higher risk of leaving.
3. Improve customer support and engagement to strengthen customer relationships.
4. Monitor customers with higher monthly charges and provide personalized retention offers.
5. Use tenure and customer service information to identify customers who may be likely to churn.
6. Regularly monitor churn trends using the dashboard and take early action.
