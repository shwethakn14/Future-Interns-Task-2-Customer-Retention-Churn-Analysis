📊 Future Interns Task 2 – Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

🎯 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 2.

The objective is to analyze customer data using Microsoft Power BI to understand customer churn, identify customer retention patterns, examine customer lifetime trends, and provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

The project includes two dashboard pages: Churn Overview and Customer Lifetime Analysis.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn and retention patterns.
- Identify customer segments that may be more likely to leave.
- Examine customer tenure and lifetime trends.
- Compare churn across different contract types.
- Analyze internet service, online security, and technical support.
- Examine payment methods and monthly charges.
- Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

📂 Dataset

Dataset: Customer Churn Dataset

The dataset contains customer information, service subscriptions, contract details, payment methods, monthly charges, tenure, and churn status.

These details help analyze customer behavior and investigate patterns associated with customers leaving or continuing a service.

📊 Dashboard Features

1️⃣ Churn Overview Dashboard

This dashboard provides an overview of customer churn and service-related patterns.

- Customer Summary: Displays customer count and key customer metrics.
- Churn Rate: Shows the percentage of customers who have left the service.
- Senior Citizen Analysis: Compares customer counts by senior citizen status.
- Online Security Analysis: Examines customer distribution by online security subscription.
- Technical Support Analysis: Compares customers with and without technical support.
- Internet Service Analysis: Shows customer distribution across internet service types.
- Payment Method Analysis: Compares customer counts across payment methods.
- Monthly Charges Analysis: Displays the distribution of monthly charges.
- Retention Rate: Presents the percentage of customers who remain with the service.

2️⃣ Customer Lifetime Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on customer tenure, lifetime patterns, and churn behavior.

- Customer Tenure Distribution: Shows how customer counts vary by tenure.
- Tenure and Churn Analysis: Compares tenure patterns for customers who churned and those who stayed.
- Average Tenure: Displays the average length of time customers have stayed with the service.
- Average Monthly Charges: Shows the average monthly amount charged to customers.
- Customer Lifetime Patterns: Highlights differences between shorter-tenure and longer-tenure customers.
- Contract and Churn Analysis: Compares churn patterns across contract types.
- Retention Opportunities: Helps identify customer groups that may need additional retention support.

💡 Key Insights

- Customer churn metrics help evaluate customer retention performance.
- Tenure analysis helps investigate whether newer customers are more likely to leave.
- Contract comparisons help reveal differences in customer churn patterns.
- Monthly charges provide another dimension for examining customer behavior.
- Service usage and payment methods help explore differences between customer groups.
- Customer segmentation can help identify groups that may benefit from targeted retention strategies.

🚀 Business Recommendations

- Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers.
- Identify customer segments with higher observed churn rates.
- Review contract options and customer needs when planning retention strategies.
- Monitor monthly charges and customer feedback to identify possible concerns.
- Improve customer support and proactively engage at-risk customers.
- Investigate whether service features are associated with better customer retention.
